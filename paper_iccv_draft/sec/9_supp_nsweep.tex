\section{Retraction horizon}
\label{supp:horizon}
At $H{=}4$ a track is confirmed or retracted within five frames ($2.5$\,s) of
its last observation, instead of at scene end (up to $40$ frames, $20$\,s).
The bound costs nothing in output --- the emitted set is byte-identical to
$H{=}\infty$ --- and downstream event traffic is unchanged by it ($161.60$
events per frame at every $H$).

\section{Control ranking robustness}
\label{supp:ranking}
Ranking the identity-budget control by mean instead of peak refined score, or
matching identities dataset-wide instead of per scene, moves its AssA by less
than $0.004$ in both association frames.

\section{Confirmation latency distribution}
\label{supp:latency}
The $N{-}1$ figure quoted for retrospective emission is a count of
\emph{observations}. Over the $150$ scenes the confirmation \emph{frame}
horizon has mean $3.29$, median $3$, $95$th percentile $7$ and maximum $10$;
at the $2$\,Hz annotated-frame rate this is a median of $1.5$\,s with a tail of
$5.0$\,s, against a constant $2$-observation horizon. It is unbounded in
principle.

\section{Indoor per-scene distribution}
\label{supp:indoor}
The per-scene label-switch reduction is $18.0$ switches at the median and
$20.1$ on average ($95\%$ CI on the mean $[18.7, 21.5]$).

\section{Confidence-accumulation control}
\label{supp:cbmot}
At the matched detection budget the accumulation control leads confirmation on
HOTA ($[{-}0.0157,{-}0.0128]$ sensor, $[{-}0.0065,{-}0.0052]$ world), DetA
($[{-}0.0805,{-}0.0645]$, $[{-}0.0162,{-}0.0131]$), IDF1
($[{-}0.0115,{-}0.0071]$, $[{-}0.0123,{-}0.0099]$) and DetRe; all intervals
exclude zero. In the world frame the control is not statistically separable
from the static threshold on any of the five metrics we resample (all
intervals span zero); in the sensor frame it is.
