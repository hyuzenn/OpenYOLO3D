\caption{\textbf{Confirmation removes the unstable warm-up rather than correcting it.}
One ScanNet200 instance (\texttt{scene0696\_02}, instance 28) over its first six observed frames.
The per-frame open-vocabulary prediction (top) disagrees with itself on the second observation, giving two label switches on a track that is otherwise a single class for all 50 observed frames.
Our $N\!=\!3$ confirmation (bottom) emits nothing until the track is confirmed, so the disagreement never reaches the map; once confirmed, the label is held.
Measured over all 17 candidate scenes, the confirmed output is identical to a suffix of the unconfirmed output for 602 of 604 instances, and \emph{no} instance has its label changed after emission --- the entire switch reduction comes from withholding the unconfirmed prefix, at a cost of two frames of latency here.
Sequences are recorded per frame from a three-arm replay; the proposal cache was regenerated, so per-scene switch counts are not bit-identical to Tab.~\ref{tab:indoor} (Sec.~\ref{supp:fig3}).}
